# CiviCRM vs Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud: Cost and Data Sovereignty Breakdown

When a non-profit organization or foundation decides to professionalize donor and supporter management, they often face a core decision: **Choose Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud or adopt an open-source solution like CiviCRM?**

While Salesforce provides an initial grant of licenses through the *Power of Us* program, many non-profits subsequently run into unexpected costs, challenges adapting to European/Spanish fiscal requirements, and severe vendor lock-in.

Here is an objective comparison of key factors for non-profit executives and technical leads.

---

## 1. Feature & Cost Comparison

| Criteria | CiviCRM | Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Licensing** | 100% Free & Open Source (GNU AGPL) | 10 free base licenses; steep recurring fees for extras |
| **Contact Limit** | Unlimited (costs scale only with server infrastructure) | Tiered pricing based on record count and data storage |
| **Spanish/EU Tax Compliance** | Native extensions for AEAT Modelo 182 and SEPA | Requires expensive custom middleware or third-party add-ons |
| **Data Sovereignty** | Total (hosted on your own private cloud or VPS) | Controlled inside Salesforce's proprietary multi-tenant cloud |
| **Local Payment Gateways** | Direct integration with Redsys, Bizum & SEPA XML | Complex setup requiring paid third-party payment bridges |
| **3-Year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)** | **Low to Medium** | **Medium to High** |

---

## 2. Hidden Cost Drivers in Proprietary CRM

1. **Storage fees**: As your supporter base and communication histories expand beyond a few thousand contacts, Salesforce storage tier upgrades increase exponentially.
2. **Consulting premiums**: Certified Salesforce developers in the non-profit sector typically charge significantly higher hourly rates than open-source specialists.
3. **Data extraction friction**: Extracting customized business logic, history, and relationships out of Salesforce in the future is technically daunting.

---

## 3. The CiviCRM Strategic Advantage

- **Full Data Ownership & GDPR Assurance**: All records remain under your direct control without dubious third-country data processing.
- **Deep CMS Integration**: CiviCRM operates seamlessly with Drupal and WordPress, powering private member portals, event registration, and donation pages without recurring software licensing fees.
